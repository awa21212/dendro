**Free, no questions asked:** personal projects, evaluation, research, teaching,
internal testing, side projects with no revenue.

**Needs a commercial license:** running Dendro as part of a product or service
you sell, ship or operate for others — including embedding it in a paid app,
offering it as a hosted/SaaS retrieval service, or bundling it into consulting
deliverables that you resell.

After **November 20, 2030** the license converts to Apache-2.0 and none of the
above applies any more.

## What a commercial license covers

- Production use in unlimited internal and customer-facing deployments.
- Redistribution as part of your product, without the BUSL notice requirement.
- Written confirmation you can show your legal team.

Not included, to be explicit: no warranty, no support SLA (a separate agreement
if you want one), no trademark rights in the "Dendro" name.

## Getting one

Open an issue titled `commercial license` on the repository (or contact
[@awa21212](https://github.com/awa21212)) and tell me:

1. Who you are and what the product is.
2. Roughly how many seats / how much volume.
3. Whether you need support, or just permission.

Small teams and single developers get a flat yearly price; tell me what you are
building and I will answer with a number, not a sales call.
